## Description

Showdown! is a native Android Pokémon Showdown! client built for the AYN Thor dual-screen handheld. It is not an emulator: it is a standalone app (Android Canvas + WebView) that connects to the official Pokémon Showdown! servers.

The client is designed around the Thor's display configuration: a 1920×1080 upper screen at 120 Hz and a 1240×1080 lower screen at 60 Hz. Battles are rendered across both screens in a readable two-screen layout, with HD-first battle and team-preview sprites, including shiny front and rear variants.

Features: live battles, two-screen playback, an optional Pokémon Battle Revolution announcer mode, searchable battle and team formats, searchable team selection, replays, rooms, tournaments, public ladders, player profiles, a team library with four move slots per Pokémon, shared team-link imports, chat, and account tools.

No ROM or BIOS dumps are required — everything runs through the official Pokémon Showdown! servers. Install the APK, sign in (or play as a guest) and battle.

## Setup guide

1. Download the APK from the repository's [latest release](https://github.com/castdrian/showdown-ds/releases/latest) (file named `showdown-<tag>.apk`).
2. Install the APK on the Thor and launch the app.
3. Sign in to your Pokémon Showdown! account or play as a guest.

See the project page: [github.com](https://github.com/castdrian/showdown-ds)
