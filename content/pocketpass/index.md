## Description

PocketPass is an app for meeting people nearby over Bluetooth and staying in touch through chat. It's built for dual-screen Android handhelds like the AYN Thor, with a single-screen layout for phones and tablets.

It recreates the fun of taking a handheld out and coming home to see who you'd met: meet people nearby, collect pieces in Puzzle Swap, fill your Bingo card, and discover regions in World Tour. There's also a friends list, private messages, and Boards for sharing notes and drawings. Your character, called a Piip (based on Miis), can be customised with hats from the Shop.

Passes are anonymous until both sides confirm and are encrypted so they can't be copied or forged.

## Setup guide

Download and install the latest release of PocketPass on your Android device. The app needs Android 11 or later.

Once installed, create your Piip and start passing people nearby over Bluetooth. The app uses both screens on the AYN Thor, with a single-screen layout for phones and tablets.

## Features

- **Passes.** Pass someone with PocketPass and your Piips show up on each other's devices, like StreetPass on the Nintendo 3DS. Passes are anonymous until both sides confirm, and encrypted so they can't be copied or forged.
- **Your Piip.** Make a Piip that looks like you, or try something completely different. Keep a few in slots, or import one with a Pretendo Network ID.
- **Profiles.** Your profile introduces you to people nearby. The people you've met stay there so you can find them again.
- **Messages.** Send a quick message, share a photo or bring everyone together in a group chat.
- **Friends.** Add the people you want to stay in touch with, see who's around and send a wave to say hello.
- **Boards.** Post a thought or share a drawing. Notes, drawings and replies, all in one shared space.
- **Games.** Weekly Bingo, Puzzle Swap and World Tour, plus a leaderboard.
- **Tokens and hats.** Earn tokens by passing people, playing games and walking. Spend them on hats for your Piip.
- **Step Rewards.** Walking earns 1 token per 400 steps, up to 25 a day.
- **Settings.** Make PocketPass feel right for you, from its look and sounds to the notifications you receive.

## Requirements

- Android 11 or later.
- Bluetooth for nearby passes.
- AYN Thor (or another dual-screen Android handheld) for the dual-screen layout; phones and tablets use a single-screen layout.
