## Description
Bluejay is a dual screen media app built around the [Grayjay](https://gitlab.futo.org/videostreaming/grayjay) core — a powerful media aggregator. It fetches content from YouTube, Twitch, BiliBili, Odysee and many other platforms, so you get pretty much everything Grayjay offers, but with a modern sheen and proper dual-screen support.

On the main screen you browse and watch videos from different platforms, manage playlists, and download content for offline viewing. The second screen of the Thor becomes a full companion panel: it can control playback, show stats, comments, the queue, recommended videos, your pinned playlists, and the home feed.

The latest update brings more dual screen features, a more consistent UI, custom color themes (light and dark), configurable player gestures, new animations throughout the app, and local watch stats tracking, along with improved stability.

Bluejay is in active development — more quality-of-life, stability, and prettification updates are on the roadmap, including local media management and music features.
