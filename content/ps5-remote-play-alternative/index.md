## Description

This is a PS5 Remote Play alternative app being developed for dual-screen devices such as the AYN Thor, and it is also expected to work with the Retroid dual-screen add-on. The app is currently in a testing phase: the developer is looking for testers to help for 14 days before releasing it on the Google Play Store. The app itself will be free with no ads, and testers will receive the premium unlock for free once the app is released.

## Features

Free features include resolution options from 540p, 720p and 1080p, 30fps/60fps, HDR, a low-latency decoder (where the device's APU supports it), multi-touch touchpad, zonal taps (left/middle/right click) and gyro support — all free and with no ads. A premium unlock adds extra features such as dual-screen support and overscan for non-16:9 screens.

## Testing

The developer is looking for testers with dual-screen devices, including the AYN Thor and the Retroid dual-screen add-on. The testing period runs for 14 days. To volunteer, reply in the comments of the announcement post or reach out via DM. Testers will be provided the premium unlock for free once the app is released.
