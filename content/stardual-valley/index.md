## Description

Stardual Valley is a dual-screen mod for Stardew Valley on the AYN Thor. It turns the second screen into a Stardew-style menu window rendered with the game's own font, tabs and clock, giving you instant access to your bag, stats, farm and more while you play. The mod has received a full visual overhaul and a large set of new features, including wallpapers and themes, a details bar, gear slots, drag-and-drop item management, mastery tracking and a seeds panel.

## Features

- Full visual overhaul: every page is now a Stardew-style menu window with the game's own font, tabs and clock
- Wallpapers and themes, including several in-game wallpapers
- Details bar on every page: day, weather, time, gold, energy and health
- Bag row that slides in when you change item or row (can be set to the bottom screen or the top screen)
- Gear slots on the Main page
- Drag and drop to move items around the bag and to equip gear
- Mastery tracking on the Progress page
- Tap a level 5 or 10 skill box to see the profession you picked
- Seeds panel on the Farm page: days to grow and the last day to plant
- Active buffs shown under your energy and health
- Bag, gold, energy and health update almost instantly
- Any panel can be folded away, including individual Community Center rooms
- Crafting lists what you can make right now first
- People page is cleaner: loved gifts are hidden by default (toggleable in Settings)
- Wiki pages no longer repeat facts or list bundles you have already filled

## Supported games

- Stardew Valley

## Setup guide

Download and install the latest release of the mod on your AYN Thor, then launch Stardew Valley. The second screen will show the Stardual Valley menu window with your bag, stats, farm and other information.
