## Description
A native Android port of Hollow Knight: Silksong with dual-screen capabilities and optional Steam integration. It compiles to native arm64 via IL2CPP with Vulkan shaders, and the porting happens entirely on the device — just install the APK and supply your own game files, either by copying them from your PC or by signing in to Steam and letting the app download them for you.

The bottom screen shows an interactive map, inventory, crests, tasks and journal while the game runs on top. Quality-of-life settings include skipping the intro, resolution control, and automatic cloud save upload/download. Any Android device works, single screen as well (tested on the AYN Thor and the Retroid Pocket Flip 2).

The app contains no game content — it is a build system that takes your own game files and compiles a playable build on your device.
