## Description

DuoFrost is a free, open-source LED control app for AYN devices, built as an independent continuation of BiFrost. It brings screen-matching colours, audio-reactive lighting and custom LED effects to AYN devices. Choose a look, organise your presets or let Smart Scenes adapt your lighting to your apps, games, time and battery. BiFrost's ambient colours, audio effects, Ambi Aurora, separate left/right controls, presets, app profiles, themes and integrations remain the foundation; DuoFrost adds background controls, brightness limits, sleep timers, temporary mute, widgets and more flexible schedules.

## Features

- **Cleaner interface:** a clear lighting dashboard, readable preset cards and direct output, mute and timer controls. Layouts adapt to narrow and wide windows, including short landscape screens.
- **Smart Scenes:** combine apps or game groups, hours, weekdays, battery and charging conditions. Choose a preset or dim the current look, see why a rule applies, and hold a temporary choice for 15–120 minutes.
- **Better preset library:** favourites, named collections and search work together. Preview saved colours and supported animations before applying them.
- **Safer sharing:** review imported presets, choose how matching names are handled and opt into app assignments. Undo one eligible import or deletion for ten minutes while no later relevant changes have been made.
- **Stability improvements:** calmer app/game switching, safer preset handling and backups that include scenes, favourites and collections.
- **Hidden feature — tidy Recent apps:** DuoFrost's window stays out of Android's Recent apps list. Reopen it from its app icon; use Start/Stop to control lighting.

## Requirements

- An AYN device. Tested on Ayn Odin 3 Pro and Ayn Thor Max; the maintainer's device is AYN Thor.
- Capture permission for ambient and audio effects. Samples are processed locally; captured content is not recorded or uploaded.
- For background lighting, allow DuoFrost to run in the background in Android's battery settings.
- After an Android Force stop, open the app again.

## Setup guide

- Export a full backup and keep it outside the app.
- Download the APK from the latest release and open it on your AYN device.
- Follow the first-run guide, choose a preset and grant only the permissions you need.
- DuoFrost 2.1.0, 2.3.0 and the 2.5.0 alphas update in place; your presets and settings are retained. Keep the backup until you have checked the update.
- Original Version 1 or Version 2 needs a one-time reinstall: export a full backup outside the app before uninstalling, uninstall the old DuoFrost, install 2.5.0, import your backup and grant the needed permissions again.
- Coming from BiFrost? DuoFrost installs alongside it. Export your presets, themes or backup from BiFrost, then import them into DuoFrost. Run one LED controller at a time.
