## Description

FTLDS is an unofficial Android port of FTL: Faster Than Light optimised for the AYN Thor's two screens. Your ship stays on the upper screen. Between encounters, the lower screen becomes your flight deck with the beacon map, shops, equipment and power controls. During combat, the enemy ship appears on the lower screen, with room targeting and beam aiming directly on the touchscreen. You can play vanilla FTL or FTL: Multiverse 5.5.1, and the launcher handles six optional add-ons, including The Renegade Collection and Forgotten Races. You'll need your own PC copy of FTL for either mode.

## Features

- Between fights, the lower screen shows the beacon map, shops, equipment and power controls.
- During fights, the enemy ship appears on the lower screen while your controls return to the top.
- Move crew by tapping a crew member or dragging a selection box across several crew, then tapping the destination room.
- Aim a weapon by selecting it and tapping a room on the enemy ship; for beams, drag from the start to the end of the beam path.
- Hold a weapon slot for about half a second to switch it off.
- Tap a system icon to add power; hold it to remove power where the system allows it.
- Answer dialogue with D-pad Up or Down and A to confirm, or tap the choice; press START for the in-game menu and B for the Back action.
- Sell equipment from the store's Sell tab by tapping an item, with your scrap balance visible on the upper screen.
- Story choices keep scrap, fuel, missile and drone-part counts visible on the upper screen.
- During a beacon jump, the lower screen switches to a streaking starfield and flashes as you arrive.
- Vanilla and Multiverse keep separate saves, and each add-on combination has its own run and unlocks.

## Requirements

- Android 8.0 or later.
- An AYN Thor — the two-screen layout is designed for it.
- Your own PC copy of FTL to supply ftl.dat.
- About 280 MB of free space for the ftl.dat copy, plus extra space for mods (the Multiverse assets download alone is about 712 MB).

## Setup guide

- On your Thor, open the Releases page and download the latest APK.
- Open the APK to install it; if Android prompts you, allow Install unknown apps for the browser or file manager you used.
- On your computer, open your installed FTL game folder and find ftl.dat (on Steam, use Manage → Browse local files). Copy the file to an easy-to-find folder on the Thor, such as Downloads.
- Launch FTL Thor and select ftl.dat in the Android file picker. The app copies it into private storage on first use.
- Choose Vanilla to start the base game, or Multiverse 5.5.1 to open mod setup.
- In Multiverse setup, tap Download missing to fetch the pinned packs, or tap Choose file to supply a pack you already have. This build expects Hyperspace 1.23.2, Multiverse 5.5 Assets and Multiverse 5.5.1 Data.
- Once setup finishes, leave all boxes clear and tap Play selected for standard Multiverse, or tick any combination of The Renegade Collection, Forgotten Races, The Outer Expansion, R&D, Lily's Beam Emporium and Judge Boon Selector. Select all enables the full catalogue.
- Returning to a run? Choose the same mode and add-on selection you used to start it.
