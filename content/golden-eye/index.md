## Features
- Weapon quick select: the bottom touch panel shows a live weapon menu — every carried weapon with its ammo count, with the currently equipped one highlighted. Tap a weapon to switch to it. The main game on the top screen is unaffected. On single-screen devices the menu simply doesn't appear; nothing else changes.
