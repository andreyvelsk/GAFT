## Description

REX Dual View is the AYN Thor-specific layout of the REX Play platform, a custom Pokémon vanilla and romhack emulation platform that makes use of the handheld's two screens. It is powered by mGBA for accuracy and is playable on iOS, Android, Steam Deck, or in a browser. It is not 3DS or official hardware.

## Features

- All features are individually toggleable on and off.
- Enhanced widescreen based on the player's location, working in the overworld and interior locations.
- 1x audio speed at 2/3/4x fast-forward.
- Match the UI to the game's palette.
- Shaders and ghosting settings.
- View deeper in-game data for your party, bag, maps and locations, with a built-in DexNav for every route and location.
- Import/export saves, Cloud Saves synced to all devices, and a Save Runs feature that allows multiple playthroughs for a single game, including save locking to prevent accidental deletion.
- Online and offline mode.
- Shared World (alpha, in development): create a lobby for a romhack and see each other's sprites in the game. No real interactions yet.

## Supported games

- All vanilla games: RBY, GSC, FRLG, RSE.
- 50+ romhacks on the REX platform, with more being added.

## Setup guide

Sign up for the beta at the project's beta signup page — an account is required for signup. After signing up, download and install the app on your supported device (iOS, Android, Steam Deck, or browser) and launch the Dual View layout on your AYN Thor.
