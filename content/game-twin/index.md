## Description

Game Twin is a second-screen companion for guides, maps, images, checklists and achievements. It gives every game its own vault: it finds walkthroughs, collectible maps, missables lists and your own checklists, files them with almost no effort, and keeps every saved copy readable offline. Switch games and your whole workspace switches with it, exactly where you left off. The app is free, ad-free, and has no analytics or trackers.

## Features

- Automatic game detection: integrates with Cocoon, ES-DE, RetroArch, RetroAchievements and Steam, and brings up the relevant content when you launch a game.
- Overlay mode for dual-screen games: shows guides and achievements directly on top of your game, or on the second screen of a dual-screen handheld such as the AYN Thor.
- Direct guide search: links straight to dedicated guides for thousands of games instead of dumping you onto a search engine.
- Guides: one tap to walkthroughs from StrategyWiki, GameFAQs, IGN, Neoseeker and Steam Community, with series-specific sources for titles such as Metroid, Zelda, Pokémon and Fire Emblem. Includes reader mode, offline saving, PDF/TXT/EPUB/HTML/MHT export, bookmarks, find-in-page and automatic table of contents.
- Images and maps: maps from VGMaps and series sites, your own markers, remembered pan and zoom per image, and screenshot capture saved straight into the vault.
- Achievements: RetroAchievements and Steam in one view, with missable and unlocked filters, offline caching, an animated unlock timeline and comparison with other players.
- Checklists: collectibles, side quests and boss strategies, color-tagged, reorderable, and importable from markdown.
- Game-aware search: the game's name is added to your query automatically, and your last ten searches, open tabs and saved pages stay attached to that game. Includes a multi-tab browser with reader mode and optional ad, tracker and cookie-banner blocking.
- Optional AI assistant: off until you enter your own Google Gemini key; you can ask about the spot you are stuck on, optionally with a screenshot of the top screen attached.
- Library and sync: artwork-rich grid and list views, external storage, and sync through Syncthing, DriveSync or any folder-sync app.
- Apps: install emulators and companion apps directly or through Obtainium, arranged for the second screen.

## Requirements

- Android 13 or newer.
- Works on any Android 13+ phone or tablet.
- On handhelds, Game Twin picks up recent games from ES-DE, Cocoon, RetroArch, RetroAchievements and Steam, with full controller support.
- On dual-screen handhelds such as the AYN Thor, the companion panel lives on the second screen while you play on the first.

## Setup guide

- Download and install the latest release from [the GitHub releases page](https://github.com/alpha-minus/game-twin/releases/latest), or add the repository to Obtainium.
- Pick a folder for your files.
- Optional: add RetroAchievements or Steam credentials, or point Settings at your ES-DE, Cocoon or RetroArch folder, for automatic game detection.
- Launch a game. Game Twin creates the library entry for you.
