## Description
AYN Thor DualScreen Mods provides native lower-screen companions for games running through WEMU on the AYN Thor. The Android companion keeps useful information and touch controls on the lower display while the game remains playable on the upper display. The current release includes the DualScreen Android app and the Stardew Valley SMAPI integration.

For Stardew Valley the companion shows a **Today** page (weekday, date, time, weather, luck, gold, energy, health, shipping, skills, hotbar and all unlocked backpack rows), a live **Map** with the current location and farmer marker, a **Farm** page with scrollable machine, animal and fruit-tree lists, a **Journal** with active quests and rewards, **Bundles** with Community Center progress, and a four-week season **Calendar** with birthdays, festivals and travelling-cart days.

The Today page places the backpack directly below the hotbar, L/R rotates every unlocked backpack row through the active hotbar, tap selects or moves items, and hold uses or eats them. Watering cans show a capacity bar, weapons show their special-action cooldown, and skills show their current level.
