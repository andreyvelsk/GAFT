## Description

FauxVkTest is a free Android app that lets you validate, test and benchmark whatever GPU driver is on your system, then upload your validation results and benchmarks to the companion website fauxvkdb.canonspike.com. The site lets you browse and compare the different driver offerings and see what each driver delivers for your gaming needs.

With many custom drivers available for Qualcomm Adreno GPUs and upcoming ARM Mali GPUs, it can be hard to navigate and understand what each driver offers. Custom drivers may not always provide better performance, but they open up possibilities for better emulation support with a wider set of Vulkan extensions compared to the much more conservative system drivers. FauxVkTest helps you check the drivers and understand what each one enables, such as Switch emulation compatibility and DirectX compatibility, all in one app.

## Features

- Validate, test and benchmark whatever driver is on your system
- Upload validation results and benchmarks to the companion website fauxvkdb.canonspike.com
- Browse and compare the different driver offerings and see what each delivers
- Works on both Adreno and Mali devices
- No root needed
- No account, no ads, no analytics
- Nothing leaves your device unless you hit Upload
- Optional one-time Supporter purchase for a badge and no more reminder; no features are gated behind money

## Requirements

- Android device with an Adreno or Mali GPU
- No root required

## Setup guide

Download and install FauxVkTest from the Google Play Store. Open the app and run the validation, test and benchmark options for the driver currently on your device. If you want to share your results, hit Upload — an upload sends your device model, chip, Android version, the driver name/version, and the test results to the companion website. Nothing leaves your device unless you press Upload.
