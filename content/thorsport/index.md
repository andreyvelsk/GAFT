## Description

ThorSport turns the AYN Thor's bottom screen into a live sports scoreboard. Everything is drawn to fill the 1240×1080 screen exactly: game cards with team logos and colors, standings, and a big arena-style Board for the game you follow. Watch a game on the TV or on the top screen while the bottom screen keeps the score, the clock and the play-by-play.

When your team scores, ThorSport plays a GOAL! / TOUCHDOWN! / HOME RUN! celebration with a goal horn, and the Thor's stick lights flash in your team's colors.

## Features

- **Leagues:** NHL, PWHL, NBA, NFL, MLB, WNBA, F1, League of Legends, Premier League, MLS, Champions League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1, plus a ★ MY TEAMS page with all your teams' games
- **Board:** arena scoreboard for one game with big logos, glowing clock, period table, scorers, last play and win chances; a live field for NFL (ball spot, first-down line, red zone), a field with lit bases for MLB, and a live shot map on an ice rink for NHL
- **Two-screen mode:** a giant Board on the top screen and scores / standings on the bottom; or keep the top screen free for the game
- **Celebrations:** goal screen with confetti, fireworks and the scorer, goal horn, and special ones for home runs, grand slams, overtime winners, shutouts and comebacks
- **Alerts:** game reminders, puck drop, power play and empty net, lead changes, hat tricks, end of period, final score, and more
- **Stick lights:** the sticks show your team's colors and flash on goals
- **Spoiler delay:** hide scores for a few seconds, or tap SYNC WITH TV the moment you see a goal on your stream to match the delay exactly
- **Standings:** one conference or division at a time in big text, with streaks and playoff lines
- **Controller:** fully usable with the D-pad and buttons (A open, B back, X / Y add a team to MY TEAMS, L1 / R1 tabs, L2 / R2 leagues)
- **English / Français**
- **Updates:** ThorSport checks GitHub and installs new versions from Settings → App update

## Setup guide

1. Download `ThorSport.apk` from the [latest release](https://github.com/CHINW3/ThorSport/releases/latest) and copy it to the Thor.
2. Open it on the Thor to install.
3. If it opens on the top screen: long-press the ThorSport icon → **Screen Launch → Bottom Screen**.
4. Pick your leagues and teams, or long-press any team later to add it to ★ MY TEAMS.
5. Tap the clock for Settings (two-screen mode, spoiler delay, goal horn, stick lights...). Settings → **Test a goal** shows the celebration.
6. To update, use **Settings → App update** (Android asks once to allow ThorSport to install apps).

## Tips

- Tap a game card to follow it on the Board; swipe ← → on the Board for the other games.
- Tap a score on the Board for big score mode.
- Long-press the clock to open the spoiler delay panel.

## Known issues

- Data comes from free, unofficial feeds (ESPN, NHL, HockeyTech, Jolpica, lolesports, Polymarket). If one changes, that part shows "No connection" and the rest keeps working.
- Another RGB app (like AmbiAnalog) can fight ThorSport for the stick lights: close it if goal flashes don't show.
