## Description

Thor Dual Screen is a free, open-source SMAPI mod that turns the AYN Thor's bottom screen into a touch companion for Stardew Valley running in Cinderbox. There is no extra app to juggle — it is just a SMAPI mod. Every feature adds something additive to the gameplay instead of being a second copy of the top screen: it does not mirror the map, repeat the HUD, or reveal anything the game hides from you. Every menu goes through the game's own code, so prices, rules and sounds are exactly the game's. The mod deliberately does not duplicate the top screen, does not change the game (no free items, money or skips), does not keep the screen awake, and never takes button focus away from the controller.

## Features

- **Today:** luck, tomorrow's weather, crops ready and dry, birthdays this week
- **Gifts:** who loves the item you're holding, and the nearest villager's hearts
- **Bag:** touch inventory with drag to rearrange, trash, and stack to chests
- **Craft:** recipes with have/need counts, one tap to craft
- **Aim:** tap the exact tile to place things or swing your tool
- **Chests, shops, shipping bin and Clint's geodes:** touch layouts while they're open — buy 1/5/25, sell any amount, pick an amount to ship, tap a geode to crack it
- **Title screen:** buttons on the bottom
- **People:** where everyone is right now
- **Tabs:** hide and reorder tabs

## Requirements

- AYN Thor (the bottom screen is the Android "presentation" display)
- Cinderbox with its SMAPI
- Your own copy of Stardew Valley from Steam or GOG — no game files ship with the mod

## Setup guide

- Download ThorDualScreen from the [latest release](https://github.com/hung-eggie-do-covergo/stardew-thor-dual-screen/releases/latest)
- Copy the folder to `/sdcard/StardewValley/desktop/Mods/ThorDualScreen/`
- Start the game from Cinderbox. The bottom screen lights up on the title screen
- Back up `/sdcard/StardewValley/desktop/Saves` before trying any new mod

See the project page: [github.com](https://github.com/hung-eggie-do-covergo/stardew-thor-dual-screen)
