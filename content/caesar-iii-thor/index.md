## Description

Caesar III Thor brings the 1998 city-builder Caesar III to the AYN Thor's two screens. It is built on Augustus, an open-source version of Caesar III that runs the original game from your own copy. The city is shown on the top screen with nothing over it, while the menus, minimap and build buttons live on the bottom screen. Messages and building info open there too, and the game works with the controller, touch, or both.

## Features

- The top screen shows the city, and nothing else.
- The bottom screen holds the rest: date, money, ratings, minimap, build buttons and menus, redrawn in HD.
- Messages and building info open below, so they never hide the city.
- The controls are mapped: sticks for the cursor and the map, triggers to zoom.
- You can play with the controller, with touch, or with both.
- Select switches to the original layout on one screen.

## Requirements

- An AYN Thor (tested on Android 13).
- Caesar III, patched to version 1.0.1.0. The CD, GOG and Steam versions work.
- Your own copy of the game — the repository and the APK contain no game files.

## Setup guide

- Download the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/simonbrtrnd/augustus-thor/releases/latest) and open it on the Thor. The app is called Caesar III Thor and installs next to the official Augustus app.
- Copy your Caesar III folder to the Thor, for example to the SD card.
- Open the app and pick that folder. Your saves go in the same folder.
