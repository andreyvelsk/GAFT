## Description

Krush Kill 'N Destroy 2: Krossfire on the AYN Thor as a native arm64 Android app built on OpenRA and OpenKrush – no Wine, no Box64, no emulation, 60 fps. The top screen shows the battlefield in full screen with no sidebar, while the bottom screen acts as the Thor deck with the complete build menu (Infantry / Vehicles / Buildings / Defense) using the original pictures from your own game files, cost in oil, progress and one queue per factory; locked or too expensive entries are greyed out. Tap to build, long-press to hold or cancel; buildings are tapped on the deck and placed on the top screen. The deck also shows the minimap (tap to jump), oil, selection, control groups and Deploy / Stop / Attack / Move / Waypoint / Sell / Repair / Menu / Save. Survivors, Evolved and Series 9 are playable in skirmish against the AI on the 15 original multiplayer maps, with the original announcer, voices, effects, music and intro videos, plus save/load.

## Features

- Native arm64 Android app built on OpenRA + OpenKrush – no Wine, no Box64, no emulation, 60 fps
- Top screen: battlefield in full screen, no sidebar
- Bottom screen (deck): complete build menu with the game's own unit and building pictures, cost in oil, progress and one queue per factory; locked or unaffordable entries are greyed out
- Deck also includes minimap (tap/drag to jump), oil, selection, control groups and Deploy, Stop, Attack, Move, Waypoint, Sell, Repair, Menu, Save
- Touch: tap = select / command, drag = selection box, two-finger drag = scroll, two-finger tap = deselect
- Controller: left stick = cursor, right stick = scroll, A = left click, B = right click / deselect, X = stop, Y = attack-move, L3 = deploy, L1/R1 = focus ring over the build cards, L2/R2 = deck tab, D-pad = groups 1–4, Select = menu, Start = Enter
- Survivors, Evolved and Series 9 in skirmish against the AI on the 15 original multiplayer maps (converted on the device)
- Original announcer, unit voices, sound effects and music; intro videos play; save and load work

## Setup guide

- You need your own copy of Krush Kill 'N Destroy 2: Krossfire on Steam (App 1292180). The app contains no game data.
- Copy the game folder "KKND 2 Krossfire" to the Thor (at least the levels, music and fmv folders).
- Install the APK from the [latest release](https://github.com/harmssi/thor-kknd2/releases/latest).
- On first start, tap "Choose Steam folder" and pick the KKND 2 Krossfire folder. About 560 MB are imported and the 15 maps are built.
- Play: Singleplayer → Skirmish, add a computer player, Start Game. Tap Deploy on the deck to set up your Outpost.

## Known issues

- No campaigns yet – skirmish only
- No aircraft
- Balance follows OpenKrush's unfinished 2020 KKND2 rules, not tuned against the original
- Only the English sound set and English video sound tracks are used
- Network play is untested
