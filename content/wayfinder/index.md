## Description

Wayfinder is a free app for the AYN Thor that makes its two screens work as one. It moves and swaps apps between the screens live — the app keeps running and keeps its state — sends the controller to the screen you want, gives every game its own buttons, and puts the Thor's settings one press away, all without leaving your game. It is described as a necessity for anyone who hasn't tried it yet, making navigation on the Thor a breeze.

## Features

Move and swap apps between the screens live — the app keeps running (hold Back). Combos on Home, Back and the AYN button for 30+ actions, with a cheat sheet when you hold Home. Game controls (Home + X): remap any button per app or per game — keys, mouse, macros, gyro. Keyboard & mouse (Home + Y) on the other screen: PC keys, a trackpad (with a Touch mode), your own pad, the game's guide. Quick panel (AYN button) over your game: brightness, volume, performance, fan, 45 shortcut tiles. Per-app profiles: screen, performance, fan, refresh rate, lights, controls. No root, no PC: one-press setup.

## Setup guide

Download the latest release from the GitHub releases page and install it on your Thor. Setup is one press — no root access and no PC required. For step-by-step help on every feature, check the project's wiki.

## Known issues

Wayfinder should not be used together with ClusterTune (CPU frequency limits). Many users reported instability and crashes when both are active, since both change how the Thor's CPU runs and can fight each other. Turn ClusterTune's limits off (or uninstall it) and use Wayfinder's own Performance settings instead. If your Thor was unstable with both, restart it after removing ClusterTune.
